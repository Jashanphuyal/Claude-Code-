# Available Brandable Domains: Outreach + Resale

Checked 2026-09-29 against the official .com registry (Verisign RDAP), with the shortlist also confirmed by DNS (no nameservers).
Spaceship blocks automated searches, so **confirm price on spaceship.com before buying**. A few may be priced as registry "premium".

## What your portfolio targets
- Two words, one email-space word + one word: `warmup*`, `inbox*`, `senders*`, `emailers*`, `opens*`, `replies*`
- Suffixes: hub / hq / kit / crew / desk / stack / labs / ninja / suite
- Friendly nouns: inboxacorn, inboxlily, jollyinbox, snowemail, navyinbox
- Best ones (SentKit, MailersKit, Aquaemail, Malify) are short and sound like a real SaaS company.

Weak spots to avoid: `warmup*` and `opens*` names tell recipients you're a cold-email or warmup operation. They're fine for tools but bad as a sender identity and hard to resell outside deliverability.
