STEP 1: READ EVERY EMAIL
Go through every email in spam1, spam2 and spam3 and keep track of each one's subject line. If the same or nearly the same email shows up twice (like a replay reminder sent twice), count it once.
An email is unusable if it is:
- blank or "None"
- 100% promotional (a sale, a launch, a webinar or event invite, a replay reminder, "seats are limited", "join here")
- a system or admin email (confirm your subscription, welcome, receipt, password, calendar invite)
- too short to have a real point

STEP 2: PICK THE BEST NUGGET (Playbook section 2)
From all usable emails, pick the best nugget, not the first one:
1. a personal story or experience that is actually theirs
2. an opinion or hot take
3. a framework or teaching with real substance
4. a funny or relatable moment
A striking client win or a singular number also counts if it is the most unique thing (the "$150K beats 15 years of experience" rule in Playbook section 5).
Use ONE email only. Never blend emails. Ignore the pitch or CTA at the end and use the value part.
Then find ONE specific detail inside it: a line they wrote, a number, a moment, a name. Not the topic.
Nugget test: what would Shardul remember tomorrow if he told a friend about this email.
If there is no usable email at all, the output is exactly: SKIPPED

STEP 3: WRITE THE 3 LINES (35-50 words, never padded)

Line 1, the opener. Always:
"I subscribed to your newsletter a while back."
(Small variations are fine: "I've been on your newsletter for a while now.")

Line 2, the story. One sentence naming the specific detail, in Shardul's own casual words:
"Loved the email where you [detail]." or "Loved the one about [detail]."
- Name the detail, not the topic (Sin 1).
- Retell it, don't copy their sentences or trademark phrases (Sin 2). Quoting one short striking line is fine.
- Serious topics: don't say "loved". Use "Your email about [detail] stayed with me."

Line 3, reaction + me-factor. This is the Playbook's "Line 2", the most important line. Follow it exactly:
- It must be LIVED, not observed. Use one of the 10 payloads in Playbook section 1.
- It must make the SAME POINT as their story, not just touch the same topic. (They watched a month then acted and made money, Shardul watched YouTube trading videos and never placed a trade. That lands. A random gym joke does not.)
- Pull from Shardul's real experience: Voice Profile section 1, the experience bank in Playbook section 3, and the extra bank below. If nothing real connects, use an honest reaction or an opinion with edge instead of forcing a parallel.
- End on the reaction. No bridge, no pitch, no summary, no "anyway". A short tie-back is fine only if it belongs to THIS email.

Never invent any other experience. Never claim kids, parenting, pregnancy or being a woman. For relationship or female-perspective topics, the girlfriend angle is allowed but never invent details about her.

HARD RULES (full lists in Voice Profile 8 and Playbook 4)
- Only facts from the chosen email. Never invent numbers, names, results or feelings.
- Serious topics (grief, health, mental health, abuse, family crisis, layoffs): zero humor, no emoji, react warmly to how they handled it.
- No em dashes, Oxford commas, semicolons, question marks, hashtags, fragment stacking, or any banned phrase from the documents.
- No filler bridges like "It reminded me of", "That made me think of", "so I always appreciate a good story about".
- One emoji max, only 😂 or 🙂. "lol" or "haha" counts as the humor move. One humor move per line.
- 6th-grade words. It has to pass the conversation test and the friend test.

Before you output, run the Self-QA in Playbook section 9 silently. Never print it.
